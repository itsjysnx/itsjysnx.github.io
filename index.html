The Challenge

The sales team was drowning in spreadsheets — weekly reports took 3 days to compile manually, data was inconsistent across regions, and leadership had no single source of truth for strategic decisions.

What I Built

An end-to-end analytics platform: from raw data ingestion through automated ETL pipelines, to a Power BI dashboard suite with 12 interactive reports covering revenue, churn, pipeline health, and regional performance.

Python ETL scripts pulling from 4 source systems into a unified PostgreSQL warehouse.

Power BI reports with DAX measures for YoY comparisons, rolling averages, and drill-through by region, product, and rep.

Automated daily refresh — leadership saw fresh numbers every morning without manual effort.

Reduced weekly reporting from ~24 hours of manual work to under 1 hour of review time.

The Outcome

Within 3 months, two underperforming regions were identified and corrective action taken — resulting in a 14% lift in quarterly revenue for those regions. The dashboard became the primary decision tool for the VP of Sales.

The Problem

A local photography studio managed all bookings through WhatsApp and a shared Google Calendar. Double-bookings happened regularly, payments were tracked in a notebook, and the owner had no revenue visibility.

What I Built

A full-stack web application with a client-facing booking portal and an owner admin dashboard — built from scratch in three months as a solo developer.

React frontend with multi-step booking: select service → choose date/time from live calendar → fill details → pay via Stripe.

Node.js + Express REST API with JWT auth, conflict detection, and automated email confirmations via Nodemailer.

PostgreSQL schema with services, packages, photographers, and time slots fully normalized.

Admin dashboard with upcoming bookings, revenue charts, and CSV export for the owner.

The Outcome

300+ bookings processed in the first 6 months with zero double-booking incidents. The owner reported saving ~8 hours per week on admin tasks.

Churn Prediction

The Brief

A SaaS company was losing ~8% of their customer base each quarter with no clear understanding of who was at risk. The customer success team only found out when customers cancelled.

My Approach

Starting from 15,000 rows of historical data across 18 months, I built a predictive pipeline to classify customers by churn risk and identify behavioral signals that preceded cancellation.

Feature engineering: login frequency, feature adoption rate, support ticket volume, days since last activity, and plan tier were all significant predictors.

Trained and evaluated four models — Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, Decision Tree — using stratified k-fold cross-validation.

Selected Logistic Regression (87% accuracy, 0.84 AUC-ROC) for its interpretability, critical for CS team action on individual predictions.

Tableau dashboard surfacing top 50 at-risk customers weekly with predicted probability and key risk factors per account.

The Impact

The CS team ran a 90-day intervention pilot on the high-risk segment. Churn in that cohort dropped 22% compared to the prior year's same period.
